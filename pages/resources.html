Peer Mentor Form Peer Mentee Form Grad Mentee Form

Undergraduate Poster Session

Cornell SPS hosts undergraduate poster sessions where all undergraduates doing research in the Physics, AEP, or Astronomy departments are encouraged to present. A panel of anonymous faculty judges decides on a best poster, awarded a small cash prize.
